Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with bLinkEdit Production.

How realistic are your 60s AI UGC ads and avatars?

Our AI UGC ads utilize proprietary face-swapping, custom avatar training, and advanced voice cloning that makes them virtually indistinguishable from real human actors. They feature natural eye blinks, micro-expressions, and native mouth sync.

What is the typical turnaround time for a 2-minute cinematic film?

Standard delivery for 2-minute cinematic masterpieces takes between 5 to 7 business days. If you select our Rush Delivery option, we expedite production and deliver polished drafts within 2 to 3 days.

Do you provide full ownership and commercial rights?

Yes, 100%. Upon final delivery and payment, you receive full commercial rights to use, scale, and run the videos across all paid ad channels (Meta, TikTok, YouTube) and organic social media without any restrictions.

What do I need to provide to get started?

We would like to get your video storyboard, raw footage or product assets if you have them, and a brief description of your goals. If you don't have a storyboard yet, our team can write it from scratch for you!
